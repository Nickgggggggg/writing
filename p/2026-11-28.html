What looksmaxxing taught me about outsized returns

Like many games we play in life, the game of improving your looks is about small things that add up

It's a 100 golden nuggets:
Your hair
Your eye area
Your skin
Your water retention
Your bone structure
Your colouring
Your behaviour...

For example:
You fix your monobrow. Nice now you look a tad bit more presentable and bearable to the human eye
You dye your eyebrows. Ok now your face is still pretty boring but there is a bit of contrast there
You make your eye brows grow thicker and denser, you shape the entire brow not just stray hairs, you clear up your scleras redness, you put on colored contacts after considering the colour theory of your eye area...

NOW the middle of your face is drawing attention from its contrast, its giving implicit directions on where to look, and it results in a strong feelling of being seen by piercing eyes waiting for the observer

The sum total effect is greater than the indivual parts which makes the return on investment exponential instead of asymptotic

BUT there is something to be catious off, these outsized returns come from synergy and balaance in the face. They have to be curated and held up against a long term vision

If the sum total effect isnt taken into consideration what might happen is a messy mix that conveys chaos and untidiness.
Which if that is the goal -> Go for it
But if it isnt you might actually be distancing yourself from your goal instead of getting closer to it
