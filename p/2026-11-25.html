The problem might NOT be the stack, the problem might just be your life

Yes your estrogen might be poorly managed, yes your thyroid might be in the dumps from dieting, yes your motivation might be blunted from sever desenstization over the past few months of non stop stims and dopaminergics.

These things might all be correct and yes you could solve all of your problems if you just had a more intelligent stack

OR

It might just be the case that the situation you find yourself in day by day is not conclusive to how youd want to live

Look at your finances - do they justifiably make you anxious?
Look at your relationships - is it reaonable they leave you empty or lacking fullfilment?
Look at your self esteem - do meet your own standards (or if you dont) do you treat yourself like you would a close friend?

Oftentimes the simplest explination is the best, so before you make an adjustment to your stack, look toward your circumstances
