Living the averge modern lifestlye is mental suicide

Your body might still exist right here, but your mind has been split into so many different fractions of scattered attention we could functionally consider it dead.

The minds vessel the body keeps existing so your mind never truly dies, but its also never truly in one piece

And everyday your impulses pull you into a new direction another part of your whole shatters and disaptes

Thus you experience something I might call the cicle of samsara regularly within your own mind

Life Death and Rebirth going on and on
