The neurological mechanism behind "burning through karma"

Burning through karma - The idea of evaporating one earhtly desire after another, sounds like a very hopeful premise to me

Reason being, if taken at face value, you could then just say that reaching a state of enlightenment is just a phasic step by step aproach much like in a video game.

The fact that this is a naive way of looking at attaining buddhahood, is a topic for another day

What I want to focus on here is thet fact that we do actually have a crucial part to how the brain learns and enacts neural plasticity on itslef that fits a "phasic" aproach quite well

Let me introduce you to a little known fact -> Any habit you build will probably stay with you for the rest of your life.

"Yeah habits are strong" you may say "but the brain has a "use it or loose" policy"

Yes - on the surface

See what happens is that while the neural plasiticy of the brain is able to and does in fact regularly prune large quantities of neurons
It is NOT the case that this happens equally across all connections

The reversal of addictions, be it nicotine, alcohol, opiods...
Doesnt truly happen just with the learned connections getting weaker
It happens by strenghing a NEW habbit on top of the existing connects that acts as the brake on the old habit

Put plainly, if you have succesfully stopped smoking, what you did is you built a new "counter addiction" that battles with your exisitng one everytime and in your case, wins on a regular basis

Now what does this have to do with burning through karma?

In order to be done with a desire fully - you have to have expereinced it (trained the bad habbit)
And then have to let it go (train a newer stronger counterforce that remains)

Thus you end up being better off than if you would have never gotten the bad habit in the first place, because now instead of still having this open loop, you are done with it

BIG CAVEAT - and this should sound obivious - this does NOT mean you should delibaretly try to get addicted to anythign, you will in fact end up worses off if you do that

I REPEAT DO NOT TAKE THIS AS A JUSTIFICATION FOR MAKING BAD LIFE CHOICES

The idea I have given you here should be use merely as a new lense form which you view your own behaviour from, where possebily, hopefully, you can gain an ounce more of optimism and sight towards betterement instead of pessimism and nihilism
