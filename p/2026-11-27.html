Are you still in love with training?

I am not talking about the ritual you have constructed around it: Drinking caffeine, blasting music and socialising in the gym.

Of course those things are sick as fuck

But when was the last time you actually went ahead and just trained.

No headphones, no talking, no caffeine

Just you your thoughts, your heavy breaths and the weight

I tell you it is profoundly beautiful to experience
